# Public User Guide

Welcome to the Jimma Islamic Council community portal. This guide explains the main public features and how to use them. You do not need a staff account to browse the public pages or use the public service forms.

Use the **User guide** link in the top bar to return here. On a phone, open the navigation menu for the main site sections.

## Find an institution or local area

1. Open **Institutions** in the main navigation.
2. Choose **Mosques**, **Madrasas & Hifz**, **Teachers & Mu’allims**, or **Ulema scholars**.
3. Search or browse the directory, then open an entry to see its available profile and contact information.
4. Choose **Explore the kebele map** to view locations and local areas geographically.

## Apply for a public service

1. Open [Public Services](/services) and browse or search the catalogue.
2. Open a service's details first. Review its eligibility, steps, required documents, fee, and estimated processing time.
3. Select **Apply** or **Proceed to Online Application** if the service is accepting online requests.
4. Complete the form and submit it. Keep the reference number shown after submission and use the same contact phone number when checking the request.

Some listed documents may need to be presented at an interview; check the service details. Online intake can be turned off for a service. If the form is unavailable, use the relevant council contact details shown on the site.

### Zakat assistance

Choose **Zakat Assistance** in the service catalogue. Enter the applicant's name, phone number, kebele, household size, and any relevant eligibility notes. After submitting, save the application reference. To check its status, open **Track Application**, enter the reference and the same phone number, and submit.

### Janazah support

Choose **Janazah Support** and provide the family contact's name and phone, the deceased person's full name, and the kebele. Select the requested support (Ghusl/shrouding, transport, or cemetery plot) and add clear location or pickup notes. Save the request reference and keep your phone available for the on-call desk. Track it using the reference and the contact phone. If online Janazah intake is unavailable, use the 24/7 hotline listed by the council.

### Other service requests

Use the service's details and application form for the instructions and required documents specific to that service. Save any reference shown. If the reference cannot be found in the tracker, contact the service desk using the details on the site.

## Events, registration, and admission passes

1. Open [Events](/events). Search by keyword or filter by category, kebele, or registration status. Open an event to review its date, time, venue, capacity, and terms.
2. If registration is open, choose **RSVP / Register** and enter the attendee's full name, phone, email, and requested registration details.
3. For a free event, submit the form. If accepted, the pass is issued and can be opened from the confirmation.
4. For a paid event, follow the event's payment instructions and upload the receipt with the registration. The registration remains pending review until the council approves payment; an admission pass is issued after approval.
5. Keep the pass number and email. To look up existing passes, use **My Passes** and provide the registration email and phone. Passes can be downloaded or printed from the pass view.

If the event is full, cancelled, or closed to registration, the portal will not accept a new registration.

## Event and browser notifications

1. On the Events page, choose **Subscribe to Alerts** or **Preferences & Filters**.
2. Select email and/or browser notifications, then choose event categories, kebeles, and reminder timing.
3. Verify your email using the link sent to you before email alerts can be delivered.
4. For browser push, allow notifications for the site, enable browser alerts, and save preferences on each device you want to use.
5. Use **Send Remote Push Test** to send a test to the currently registered browser only. A success message means the push service accepted the request; check that device's notification center for the alert.

You can update preferences or remove your subscription from the same preference panel. Browser permission can also be changed in your browser's site settings.

## Donations and Zakat calculation

### Make a donation

1. Open [Donate](/donate), select a fund and amount, and enter donor details. You may choose to give anonymously.
2. Select a listed payment method and read its displayed transfer instructions.
3. Confirm the donation request and save the reference number.
4. Complete the transfer separately using the displayed instructions and keep your payment evidence. The portal records a request; its confirmation is **not** a payment receipt or proof that payment has been verified. Wait for the council to confirm the payment.

### Calculate Zakat or Ushr

1. Open the **Zakat Calculator** from the top bar, the Services page, or [Donate](/donate?tab=zakat-calculator).
2. Enter the relevant amounts and quantities for your cash, bank and digital balances, gold or silver, business assets, investments, crops, or livestock, along with applicable short-term debts and immediate expenses.
3. Review the displayed Nisab and calculation results. Use the calculator's Shari'ah guidance for definitions and assumptions.
4. To save an assessment to your history and load it later, sign in to a user account. Browsing and calculating do not require sign-in.

The calculator is an aid for estimation, not a payment, official application, or personal religious ruling. Ask a qualified scholar or the council's Zakat desk if you are unsure how to classify an asset.

## Announcements and Waqf information

- Open [Announcements](/announcements) to search notices and filter by priority. Use **Share** to copy the page link.
- Open **Waqf transparency** from the Services menu to review the information published in the Waqf registry.

## Contact, language, and accessibility

- Open [Contact](/contact) for the council's contact desks and frequently asked questions. Use the displayed council contact channels for follow-up.
- Use the language selector in the header to choose an available language. Use the theme control to switch between light and dark appearance.
- The staff sign-in link is for authorized council personnel. Public services, directories, events, announcements, and the calculator are available without staff sign-in.

## Keep your references safe

Save application references, payment references, event pass numbers, and the phone/email used on each request. Do not share private email-verification links or notification management links with others.
